helps every single one: v1 -inf→0.112, v2 0.169→0.396, v3 -inf→0.659. That

consistent sign across the three real champions checked — this closes out
the "third data point" question the 18:54 UTC entry opened. Reinforces
rather than changes that entry's scoping conclusion: any real gene/threshold
change should touch `consult_moderate`'s exit only. `consult_risky`'s exit
must not be bundled into the same change; its effect is genome-specific and
in v2's case actively harmful.

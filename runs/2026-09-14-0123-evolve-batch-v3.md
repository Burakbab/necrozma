batch is a useful contrast point to the last several batches (which added

pattern) — a lively best-of-generation ratio does not reliably predict how
many candidates actually reach the sealed-holdout stage. No further action
taken on this observation this cycle; flagging it in case a future session
wants to correlate the two numbers across more batches before drawing a
general conclusion.

Hit a variant of AGENTS.md item 9's warning while verifying the post-batch

`&` *and* the tool's own `run_in_background: true` on a later retry of the

covers the exact failure mode and `tools/background_runner.py` already
exists as the mechanical fix for the actual `evolve` call (which was run
correctly, via `start`/`wait`, with no issue). Future sessions: use
`tools/background_runner.py` (not a raw backgrounded Bash command) for any
long-running verification step too, not just `evolve`, if there's a chance
it will outlast the tool's own foreground timeout.
